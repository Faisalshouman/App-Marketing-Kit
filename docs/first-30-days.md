# First 30 days: zero-ad-spend online acquisition experiment

This is an execution hypothesis, **not** a customer forecast.

**Week 1 — Ground truth.** Product Marketer validates working product example, current CTAs, primary user persona, competitor/customer pain; Brand Lead audits actual current website and logo; Growth Ops defines activation event and reporting. Head selects one concept.

**Week 2 — Creative production.** Copywriter finalizes a short script, Video Producer produces *actual* captured demo, Graphic Designer makes carousel, Brand Lead reviews; founder approves exact final assets and channels.

**Week 3 — Small distribution.** Social Manager posts approved assets to the legitimate channels. Partnerships researches a small set of relevant audience communities and proposes valuable online collaborations. No bulk DM automation or fake testimonials.

**Week 4 — Learn.** Growth Ops records verified clicks, real signup/activation when instrumented; Lifecycle Manager improves opt-in onboarding sequence if available; Head assesses content ideas to repeat or drop.

Suggested workload: start with 2 short videos, 2–3 graphics and 1 useful article per week *once the production workflow is proven*. Quality and product authenticity outrank volume.
