# SOP — Weekly growth reporting

Owner: `growth-ops`.

Use the period and source for each number. Distinguish raw visits, qualified visits, sample viewers, signups, first-value activations, retained active users and purchases (only when enabled). Do not sum overlapping cohorts as unique people.

For every campaign report: ID, channels, dates, UTM source/medium/campaign, actual confirmed publish URLs, verified reach/impressions if provider available, clicks, signups, activated users, attribution uncertainty, cost (if approved) and qualitative learnings.

At low traffic, prefer qualitative observation and directional comparisons over false statistical confidence. Mark missing data as `not instrumented`, not `0`. Share next experiment with Head of Marketing.
