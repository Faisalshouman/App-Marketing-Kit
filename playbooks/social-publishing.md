# SOP — Cross-channel social distribution

Owner: `social-community-manager`. Publishing and commenting are **manual by default**.

1. Read approved caption, asset and target platform. Validate account authority, platform policy, user permissions and content rights.
2. Adapt aspect ratio, caption length, hashtags, alt text and links for each intended channel without changing core facts.
3. Show founder the **actual** post package and publishing target. A brand review alone does not authorize publication.
4. If authorized integration exists, publish via official API; otherwise produce a ready-to-post human handoff.
5. Record verified post URL/receipt and time; only then mark task published. Never invent reach or engagement.
6. Review comments and engage only where appropriate and permitted; do not auto-DM community members, impersonate users, or mass reply.
7. Forward aggregate channel outcomes to Growth Ops; separate clicks from activated users.
