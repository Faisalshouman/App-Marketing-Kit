# What the five thumbnail videos support
Evidence audit · 22 November 2026

Reviewed the five linked YouTube caption tracks. The timestamps below group substantive claims and production steps. Automatic captions can misrender words and names. Visual examples are described as the presenters explain them. Private analytics, commercial claims, and audience reactions cited by the presenters remain self-reports unless independently corroborated. The audit uses four verdicts.

| Verdict | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Supported | Directly corroborated within the scope of the claim. Platform guidance supports a production practice within that platform, without proving a causal lift. |
| Conditional | A related study or plausible mechanism supports testing the claim. Effects depend on the treatment, audience, surface, or content. |
| Heuristic | A practical creator technique. Evidence here gives no general performance estimate. |
| Unverified | An uncorroborated result, causal attribution, universal claim, or exact cutoff. |

## Evidence behind the production rules

| Decision | What we know | How it enters the documents |
|---|---|---|
| Identify the image subject at actual size | Visual crowding can make peripheral objects harder to recognize. Visual search depends on both features and the viewer's goal. This research supplies no exact focal-element count or pixel threshold. [Crowding review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3070834/) · [Guided Search 6.0](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8965574/) | Require a readable subject and component-by-component inspection with a crop and masks. |
| Align title and image, then choose how much to reveal | Cho et al.'s working paper reports 10,098 videos from 233 creators and a preregistered experiment with 996 participants. Image–title agreement improved curiosity and choice in the experiment. Closing the gap with matching overlay text lacked the same experimental benefit. The observational views result is a different endpoint. [Author abstract](https://iameunsol.com/research/) · [SSRN](https://ssrn.com/abstract=7543822) | Grade the expected subject and outcome separately. Test the relevance of curiosity to the route instead of requiring it everywhere. |
| Show early proof and honor the promise | Yang and Netzer randomized thumbnail exposure on an experimental video platform and modeled choice and watch time. Their working paper describes an association between preview divergence and earlier exits. The full PDF was unavailable for independent inspection in this review. The author abstract supports the design and overall result. [Author abstract](https://www.jasmineyang.marketing/research) | Require an opening confirmation and evidence for the promised payoff. Log watch-time measures alongside thumbnail conversion where available. |
| Keep face and emotion choices contextual | Gaze experiments measure attention. The curiosity MRI study linked resting-state connectivity to reported information seeking about COVID-19. These outcomes offer indirect evidence for thumbnail decisions. [Face-attention study](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/m51f6-vk333) · [Curiosity study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35975900/) | No brain-region, dopamine, universal emotion, or mandatory face criterion. |
| Choose metrics by placement | YouTube documents registered impressions, watch time from impressions, Shorts engagement, and concurrent tests for eligible non-Shorts. TikTok documents cover previews on profile/search, and watch or skip signals in its feeds. [YouTube impressions](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314486) · [YouTube format analytics](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12220281) · [YouTube tests](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400) · [TikTok covers](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/editing-posting-and-deleting) · [TikTok feed](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/exploring-videos/how-tiktok-recommends-content) | Keep asset, surface, denominator, traffic mix and success metric in the record. |

## What improvement could mean

The screenshots show **3.3% across 1,689 registered impressions** in a mixed view. The following scenarios translate the requested lifts from that baseline. Treat them as arithmetic and experiment-planning illustrations.

| Relative lift | New CTR | Absolute gain | Approximate impressions per arm |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| +10% | 3.63% | +0.33 percentage points | 48,216 |
| +20% | 3.96% | +0.66 percentage points | 12,606 |
| +50% | 4.95% | +1.65 percentage points | 2,280 |
| +70% | 5.61% | +2.31 percentage points | 1,252 |

Approximate counts use one balanced two-arm test comparing independent exposure outcomes, a fixed horizon, a two-sided 5% significance level and 80% power. They are approximate and may vary slightly with rounding. Account for repeated viewers, heterogeneous traffic, multiple comparisons and changing audience mix when interpreting real data. YouTube native watch-time-share tests use their own stopping and result rules. At a hypothetical true CTR of 3.3%, zero conversions in 25 independent impressions has probability about 43%. The ranking of thumbnails or formats in this dashboard may move substantially with another batch of exposures. [Two-proportion planning](https://online.stat.psu.edu/stat509/Lesson08) · [YouTube CTR FAQ](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7628154) · [YouTube testing](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400)

The rubric's review thresholds are internal defaults. Before inferring a quality-score-to-CTR relationship, compare multiple consistently graded releases against their controls and record how often the grades and the audience choices agree. Freeze the rubric version during that evaluation.

## What to automate and what to leave open

Automate asset extraction, variant rendering, crop simulation, layout checks, evidence logging, and score calculation. Give the reviewer an actual rendered card and record the viewer expectation before showing the brief. Put identity, attribution, and ambiguous visuals in review when the source cannot resolve them.

Let the agent decide whether the subject is a person, an object, an action, a result, or a diagram. Give it two substantially different concepts to develop. A recognizable streamer may be the strongest subject for a personality-driven clip. An explanatory animation may need a single visual relationship. A search tutorial may show the result immediately. These choices should follow from the promised experience and footage for that upload.

The earliest success criterion is fewer wrong expectations and fewer unreadable cards in a blind review. The next criterion is an audience outcome on the intended surface. Track both. A successful early check can justify publishing a test candidate while audience response is still unknown.
