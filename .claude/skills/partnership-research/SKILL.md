# Partnership research skill

1. Read `playbooks/outreach-and-partnerships.md`, `policy/claims-and-consent.md`, the selected ICP and real demo links.
2. Identify relevant organizations, educational creators or newsletters using permitted public material; record URLs, dates, fit and specific mutual benefit.
3. Write a **small ranked shortlist** with sourced rationale, not a huge scraped contact list. Keep personal contact information out of Git.
4. Draft proposals using `templates/outreach-proposal.md`, with accurate Philocoursa claims and no promised payment or perks.
5. Obtain explicit founder review and legal/channel permissions before any message is sent. If no sender integration, mark "not sent".
