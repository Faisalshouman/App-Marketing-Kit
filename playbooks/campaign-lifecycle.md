# SOP — End-to-end marketing campaign

**Trigger:** Head of Marketing approves a specific audience problem and activation objective.

1. **Evidence:** Product Marketer records customer problem, source, date, confidence and public-safe product capabilities. No invented data.
2. **Brief:** Content Strategist writes one message, intended role, channel, CTA and proof asset. Head resolves conflicting priorities.
3. **Script and text:** Copywriter prepares exact script, captions and claims checks; Website SEO creates landing copy proposal only if needed.
4. **Production:** Graphic Designer creates static assets; Video Producer plans, captures real UI, edits and exports; no fake app screenshots.
5. **Review:** Brand Lead checks actual files and visual states. Product Marketer checks facts, Growth Ops checks tracking.
6. **Founder approval:** Founder views the final exact content, channel, audience and any planned spending/sending. No silent approval.
7. **Publish:** Social Manager performs an authorized action or provides a handoff package. Record provider receipt/URL; absence of evidence is not success.
8. **Measure:** Growth Ops documents time window and actual counts (qualified visits, activation), plus limitations; Head makes stop/continue/change decision.

**Definition of done:** Approved source artifacts, actual distribution evidence where relevant, recorded metrics or explicitly missing data, and a reusable learning note. Internal production may finish without publishing, but must not be called "published".
