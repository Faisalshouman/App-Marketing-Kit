# Creator clip brief

## Intake

- Work item and accountable Video Producer
- Independent channel and intended audience
- Format and channel goal
- Human-selected source and moment or source timecode
- Completed recording reference and capture method (human-saved OBS replay or human-supplied recording)
- Approved private storage location and retention window

## Reuse permissions

- Partnerships owner and private evidence reference or rights ID
- Allowed platforms, attribution and credit placement
- Monetization terms, edit or duration limitations, expiry and revocation terms
- Clearance result for embedded music, guest appearances, gameplay or other third-party material
- Outstanding rights or privacy questions and clearance owner

## Production

- Editorial angle and context to preserve
- Hook, title, description and optional commentary
- Creative edit notes on cut points, pacing, framing, audio and caption treatment
- Output format, subtitle format and thumbnail brief if needed
- Source, render, subtitle, metadata and thumbnail version references
- Video Producer creative QA and Brand Lead review results
- Technical QA reference if tooling was involved

## Release

- Destination platform and verified channel ID or handle
- Approved visibility and schedule with time zone
- Founder approval reference for the exact version, channel and action
- Social owner, provider receipt and verified published URL when applicable
- Current status and blocker or next accountable owner

## Measurement

- Reporting window, evidence source, and reach, retention, engaged views and subscriber change definitions
- Per-clip cost and cost assumptions

Record references to private materials and approvals. Store the originals, credentials and source media in approved private systems.
