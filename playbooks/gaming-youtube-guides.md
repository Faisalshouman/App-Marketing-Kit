## From one question to a usable guide

1. **Content Strategist** records the exact question, one public demand signal, and what the guide can add. For recurring questions, compare similar videos within a channel and consider their format and age before treating a view-count difference as meaningful.
2. **Human operator and Video Producer** reproduce the answer in the supported game version on the intended platform. Record relevant prerequisites and the exact timestamps that show the starting state, key action and result. Use footage the operator supplies with the necessary clearance. Label spoiler scope and distinguish observed gameplay from interpretation.
3. **Copywriter and Video Producer** state the answer early, then show the steps and the result. Match narration to the on-screen action. Ask whether a first-time player could follow the walkthrough with the information shown. Use the [thumbnail reference](../skills/design-short-form-videos/references/youtube-thumbnail-review.md) if packaging is in scope.
4. **Partnerships and the release owner** check footage provenance and reuse scope, including publisher terms, other creators' recordings, and captured music. Rockstar's fan policy addresses occasional non-commercial use. YouTube's gameplay and channel policies are separate checks for monetization, including original value and rights. Record the basis for commercial use before a monetized release. [Rockstar fan footage policy](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/7bNaeoMFTV0iUDGhStTXvz/policy-on-posting-copyrighted-rockstar-games-material) · [YouTube gameplay monetization](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/138161) · [Channel monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392)
5. **Growth Ops** reviews the formats against their own goals. For guides, check YouTube Search traffic, retention through the demonstrated solution, and comments reporting whether the steps worked. For Shorts, use the [short-form research skill](../skills/short-form-clip-research/SKILL.md) and compare like formats. Use the channel's own data to judge revenue or reach expectations.
