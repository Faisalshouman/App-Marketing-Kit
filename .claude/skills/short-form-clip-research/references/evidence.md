## Details that matter when cutting and measuring

- In March 2025, YouTube changed its public Shorts view count to count starts and replays without a minimum watch time. It retained the earlier engagement measure as engaged views. Record which definition was used and avoid mixing historical counts across definitions as if they were identical. [Shorts view count change](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10059070?hl=en) · [Metric definitions](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12220281?hl=en)
- YouTube's automatically highlighted retention moments have eligibility conditions, including at least 60 seconds and 100 views. Use the graph only if accessible, and inspect the clip before attributing a dip to any specific edit. [Audience retention](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415?hl=en)
- Record clearance for third-party footage and audio. Ownership, permission, suitable licensing, and applicable exceptions are distinct possible grounds. A public source URL establishes provenance. Determine whether the contemplated reuse is cleared separately. [Copyright on YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2797466?hl=en)
