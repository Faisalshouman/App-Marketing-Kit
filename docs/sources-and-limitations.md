# Evidence and scope

Record the date, product version, environment and source behind each claim. Check actual user-facing behavior and offers for the audience, region and plan in scope. A roadmap, a screenshot mockup or source code alone cannot establish current public availability.

The kit includes draft workflows and local workboard tooling. Validate integrations, platform policies, product availability, consent, and pricing in the receiving workspace before use. Keep real credentials and customer records in the receiving organization’s approved systems.
