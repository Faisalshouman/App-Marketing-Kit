# TikTok content research

1. Define the channel, intended audience, language, market, and desired behavior. Choose a primary discovery surface. Search serves a stated question. For You serves discovery and needs an understandable opening for viewers who have no prior context.
2. Collect demand evidence and search coverage using the [source and sampling guide](references/evidence-and-sampling.md). Record the exact query, date, locale, source, account context where relevant, and material limitations. Use the target account's Creator Search Insights and approved TikTok Studio data when accessible. Use manual public search and anonymized question themes as fallbacks.
3. For each candidate, state what the audience is trying to learn or do and what existing answers leave out. Compare the exact query with a plain-language variant. Inspect several distinct relevant results and retain the strongest counterexample. Check actual answers through playback or captions. Mark any claim about missing visual proof provisional when playback is unavailable. A trend, search label, or standout post starts the investigation.
4. Give the candidate a disposition. **Ready to test** requires credible evidence of the question, a specific omission in the observed coverage and a feasible, audience-relevant answer the team can verify and clear. **Investigate** covers missing demand, coverage, or production evidence. **Park** when existing answers satisfy the intent, the channel cannot add value, or the premise is out of scope. Rank ready candidates by demand evidence, size of the observed omission, fit, and production effort. Use the [starter topic examples](references/starter-topics.md) to see how to document leads without treating them as verified platform gaps.
5. Hand off the exact question, sources and observation scope, gap, natural-language opening, proof beat, selected format, planned metric and review window. Use `design-short-form-videos` for short video design and `thumbnail-quality` whenever producing a cover. The human selects and supplies independent creator footage. Keep the source, releases and approval evidence with the relevant workflow.
6. When an approved post has results, compare it against similar posts from the same account at the same post age. For Search, use search-attributed views or the search metric actually available in the account, with the metric label and denominator as displayed. For For You, use feed-attributed traffic and watch or completion metrics when available. Label whole-post metrics as such when no feed breakdown exists. Keep a topic test separate from a hook or format test, and treat comparisons across posts as observational. Record limitations and the next decision.

Return a shortlist or one actionable brief, depending on the request. If only public evidence is available, state which account-specific data and search observations remain unresolved.
