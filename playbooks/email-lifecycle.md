# SOP — Permission-aware lifecycle email

Owner: `lifecycle-crm`.

1. Document trigger event, audience purpose, source of valid consent/legal basis, suppression conditions and frequency cap.
2. Draft one useful action per message (see product example, resume a saved task, complete a first workflow), with truthful current product functionality.
3. Provide clear sender identity and unsubscribe controls consistent with law and chosen provider.
4. Obtain founder approval for exact content, target audience and sending schedule before enabling broadcast/automation.
5. Send only through approved account and verify provider receipts; no list of raw recipient emails in repo.
6. Track delivery, activation, unsubscribes and complaints using aggregate numbers and explicit periods.
7. Stop messages upon unsubscribe or bounce; do not assume free/beta users can buy when paid checkout is inactive.
