# SOP — Brand and fact QA

Owner: `brand-lead` with `product-marketer` for factual claims.

For each asset record: source path, content version, channels, real rendered previews, logo reference, background/foreground pairs and contrast result, status of product claims, CTA target, language proofread, rights/source records, reviewer notes, severity and re-review.

Block on: fabricated feature, fake app screen, unauthorized logo, unverifiable testimonial, absent important rights, unusable contrast, misleading purchase CTA, privacy leakage or unreadable subtitles. Distinguish critical failure from optional aesthetic polish. Produce `templates/brand-review.md` review records. The lead cannot replace founder's public-action approval.
