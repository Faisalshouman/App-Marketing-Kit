## Evidence and adoption

All 91 claim records were reviewed for scope and relevance. The 40 production inferences remain editorial proposals. For the 51 other records, the checks below compare claims with the sources' stated tasks, source documentation, vendor reporting or mathematical assumptions. This was a claim review at the level of relevant source text and worked derivations. Replication of the research, exhaustive review of full papers and instrumented audience tests would provide different evidence.

The 30 source records, 20 unsupported-claim entries, 10 language templates, 9 recipes, 77 checks, 12 diagnostics, 6 tutorial entries, 6 selection entries, 5 claim protocols and the storyboard are documented in the source archive.

A local rehearsal exercised the media review outside the bundled validator. FFmpeg 9.0.2 decoded a synthetic 1-second H.264/AAC loop to 24 evenly timed frames and 48,000 audio samples. Motion across the intended wrap was 8.062 pixels, in line with adjacent steps. A held-endpoint control had identical stream timing and zero motion across the wrap even though compression left its two decoded boundary frames with different pixel buffers. FFmpeg applied AAC priming and trailing trim in this export. These are frame, timing and waveform observations. Perceptual listening, destination playback and audience response remain open for an actual cut.
