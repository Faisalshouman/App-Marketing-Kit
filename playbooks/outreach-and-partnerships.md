# SOP — Online partnerships and appropriate outreach

Owner: `partnerships-outreach`.

1. Start from ICP and verified product proof.
2. Research **organizations** and relevant public professional channels, noting relevance and source; no scraping prohibited/private data.
3. Prioritize reciprocal value: genuinely useful online demo, co-created workflow guide or relevant newsletter contribution.
4. Prepare a specific partnership proposal; avoid generic mail-merge, fake compliments, artificial scarcity or unsupported incentives.
5. Ask founder to approve target, message, channel, legal basis and intended recipient scope before sending. Use approved CRM for contact details; not in Git.
6. Respect opt-out, response preferences and platform policies. Record next step without spamming.
7. Measure partner-referred qualified visits and activated users; do not report speculative reach as results.
