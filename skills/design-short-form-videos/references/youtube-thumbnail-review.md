# YouTube thumbnail design and review

Source reviewed on 2026-11-21 using the English transcript displayed on YouTube. Aprilynne Alter describes recurring feedback from her own thumbnail reviews and demonstrates it with examples. The tutorial contains a sponsored tool segment. Its title's “81% better” figure has no defined metric, comparison, or result in the transcript. ## Handoff and test

1. Write the viewer's question, the intended feeling if relevant, and the clip moment or evidence that fulfills the promise. For gameplay, use the real subject or action. For podcasts, verify who said what and preserve context. For product videos, use the actual product state.
2. Choose the focal subject and a relevant reference mechanism. Record the source asset and the reason each remaining visual element is there. Preview the title and image together, then briefly hide the title. Check the smallest relevant view and, where TV matters, the actual TV placement or a clearly labeled half-frame simulation.
3. For eligible long-form uploads, Growth Ops can propose a concurrent YouTube Studio test with up to three variants. Change one element to learn its effect, or compare distinctly different packages when choosing among complete concepts. Read the platform's watch-time-share outcome and record when it is inconclusive. Use CTR diagnostically with impressions and traffic source. [A/B test eligibility and outcomes](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400) · [CTR interpretation](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7628154)
4. For Shorts, confirm the actual thumbnail controls available to the channel. YouTube documents frame selection in the mobile app and desktop Studio uploads for verified accounts. Native title and thumbnail A/B testing excludes Shorts. Keep thumbnail review distinct from in-feed opening and retention review. A comparison across different Shorts can suggest what to try next, with topic, audience and distribution recorded as confounders. [Shorts frame selection](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10343433) · [Desktop thumbnail uploads](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431?hl=en-GBAnd) · [A/B test eligibility](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400)

Record the title, candidate image or frame, promised payoff and timestamp, surface preview, changes between variants, and the test question if applicable.
